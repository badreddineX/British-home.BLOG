# Pinterest Pins — QA & Fix Sheet (UK)

## 🔴 Critical — fix before this pin gets more reach

### 1. `bedroom-colour-ideas-A` — framed photo shows the Brooklyn Bridge / Manhattan skyline
The bed's headboard wall has a large framed print unmistakably showing the Brooklyn Bridge and Manhattan skyline (New York). This pin is titled "Bedroom Colour Ideas UK" — a direct, visible contradiction. Same category of issue as Canada's Denver Broncos stadium pin.
**Fix:** swap the photo for a UK-appropriate bedroom, or at minimum one with no identifiable non-UK landmark art.

### 2. `cosy-bedroom-ideas-A` — this is a hotel room, not a home
Visible tells: a coffee/tea amenities tray on a stand, a wall-mounted air conditioning unit, a laptop open on a small round table — classic hotel room staging, not someone's actual bedroom. The blog's whole positioning is "real homes, real budgets" — a hotel room directly undercuts that.
**Fix:** replace with a genuine home bedroom photo, ideally one that doesn't read as a rental/serviced-apartment either.

### 3. `kitchen-on-a-budget-A` — American kitchen styling, too high-end for "budget"
A "Bless This Home" sign (a distinctly American decor phrase/style), farmhouse-style faucet, and granite-look counters read as an expensive American kitchen, not a UK budget kitchen. Two problems stacked: wrong-country styling AND too luxurious for the "budget" claim in the title.
**Fix:** swap for a modest UK kitchen photo, ideally one that actually looks budget-tier.

## 🟡 Medium — worth a look, less certain

### 4. `christmas-home-decor-A` — American Christmas styling on a "British Style" pin
Buffalo-plaid stocking pattern and a vintage red toy pickup truck read more Americana-Christmas than distinctly British, despite the pin's "British Style" kicker. Less clear-cut than #1-3 — Christmas decor aesthetics are fairly universal — but worth a second look given the explicit British-style claim.
**Fix:** optional. Consider a photo with more identifiably British Christmas markers (or drop the "British Style" kicker if the photo stays).

### 5. `rented-flat-makeover-A` — too luxurious for "deposit-safe" rental framing
High-gloss stretch ceiling, recessed lighting, glass dining table. Reads as an expensive, high-end renovation, not a typical rented flat a tenant would need "deposit-safe" tips for. Same mismatch pattern flagged repeatedly in the Canada blog's QA (photo too aspirational for the post's actual premise).
**Fix:** optional. A more modest rental interior would match the "deposit-safe" premise better.

### 6. `rented-home-decor-A` — North American loft aesthetic, not UK rental housing
Exposed brick with visible high-rise buildings through the window is a converted-warehouse-loft look (Chicago/NYC style), not typical UK rental housing (period conversions, Victorian terraces, modest new-builds). Mismatched with the "UK" framing.
**Fix:** optional. Swap for a photo that reads as UK housing stock.

### 7. `maximalist-living-room-A` — wall-mounted AC/heat-pump unit visible
Unusual for a "British Homes" pin — UK homes overwhelmingly use radiators, not wall-mounted AC/heat-pump units, which are far more common in mainland Europe/Asia. Minor detail, easy to miss, but a real authenticity tell.
**Fix:** optional, low priority. Could crop the unit out or swap the photo.
