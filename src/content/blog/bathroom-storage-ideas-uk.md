A small UK bathroom has almost no floor space to spare, which means storage has to come from the walls, the door, and the gap above the toilet, with no drilling into the tiles — adhesive pads, suction cups, and freestanding units cover nearly everything a [small bathroom](/blog/small-bathroom-ideas-uk) needs.

Below is a practical set of ideas organised by where the space actually is, not by what looks good on a shelf.

---

## Start With the Gap Above the Toilet

The vertical space above a toilet cistern is almost always empty in a small UK bathroom, and it's the single biggest storage opportunity in the room. A freestanding over-toilet ladder unit (£30-£55, Dunelm or Amazon) straddles the cistern with no fixings at all, adding two or three shelves of storage where there was previously nothing.

## Storage That Needs No Drilling

**Adhesive-pad and suction baskets (£8-£18 each)** hold shampoo, razors, and sponges directly on tiled walls in the shower or by the sink. Choose pads specifically rated for tile and glass rather than generic adhesive strips, since smooth tile is a harder surface to bond to than painted plaster.

**Over-door towel rails or hook rails (£10-£25)** use the back of the bathroom door, a surface almost every bathroom has and almost nobody uses, for hanging towels or dressing gowns.

**A freestanding ladder-style towel rack (£25-£45)** works in any spare corner and doubles as drying space for damp towels rather than just storage for dry ones.

**Tension-rod shelving (£25-£45)**, the same kind used in living rooms and bedrooms, works equally well wedged into a bathroom alcove or beside a sink unit — see [vertical storage ideas for small UK flats](/blog/vertical-storage-ideas-uk-flats/) for the full range of tension-rod and no-drill shelving options.

## Under the Sink and in the Cabinet

**Stackable clear boxes (£3-£8 each)** inside an under-sink cabinet stop loose bottles and cleaning products from becoming an unsorted pile, and let you see what's actually in there without pulling everything out.

**A tension-rod riser shelf** inside a cabinet doubles the usable height under a sink, since most of that space is currently empty air above whatever sits on the cabinet floor.

## Keep It Organised, Not Just Stored

A set of adhesive-pad baskets sorted by category — daily essentials by the sink, shower items in the shower, spares under the sink — costs under £40 total for a full bathroom and makes a bigger visible difference than any single larger purchase, because it stops items migrating into a single cluttered pile.
