The most common styling mistake in UK living rooms is a curtain pole fitted just above the window frame, with curtains that stop at the sill. It makes the window — and the whole wall — look shorter.

This week, if you can put two screws in a wall, **rehang the pole**:

- Fix it **10–15cm above the frame**, ideally close to the ceiling.
- Extend it **15–20cm past each side** of the window so the open curtains sit against the wall, not covering the glass.
- Curtains should **just brush the floor** — measure from the new pole height, not the old one. Most high-street panels come in 137/183/228cm drops; size up and take up the hem if needed.

Renting and can't drill? A tension rod inside the reveal plus a stick-on valance above fakes most of the effect. The room instantly reads taller.
