Standard advice says paint a small room white to make it feel bigger. In a UK box room it usually just makes it feel like a small white room.

This week, try the opposite on the smallest room in the house — the box bedroom, the loo, a home office:

- Pick a **deep, warm colour** — an inky blue, a forest green, a dark clay. Farrow & Ball's Hague Blue and Studio Green, or the cheaper matches at B&Q and Wilko.
- **Paint everything one colour** — walls, ceiling, woodwork. Losing the edges of the room is what makes it feel enveloping instead of cramped.
- One 2.5L tin (£20–£30) covers a box room with a coat to spare.

A dark small room reads as deliberate and cosy. A pale one just reads as small. If you rent, do it on moving-out terms — most landlords are fine with a repaint back.
