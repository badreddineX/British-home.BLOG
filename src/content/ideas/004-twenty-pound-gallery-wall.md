An empty wall above the sofa or bed is the thing that makes a room feel unfinished. You can fix it this weekend for £20 without a single hole.

The plan:

- **5–7 frames**, mixed sizes, one colour (black or natural wood). Charity shops and IKEA seconds — £1–£4 each.
- **Fill them free.** Print old photos in black and white, cut pages from an art book, frame a nice tote bag or a square of wallpaper. Consistency of *frame* matters far more than what's inside.
- **Hang with Command picture strips** (£6 a pack). Lay the arrangement on the floor first, photograph it, then copy it onto the wall.

Keep the outer edges roughly rectangular and the gaps even — about 5 cm. That's the whole trick. It reads as deliberate, and it comes down clean.
