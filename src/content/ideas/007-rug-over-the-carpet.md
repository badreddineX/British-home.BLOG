Rented living rooms almost always come with a beige or grey carpet that fights whatever you put in the room. You can't replace it — but you can cover the bit that matters.

This week, **layer a rug over it**:

- Go **large** — big enough that the front legs of the sofa sit on it. A rug that floats in the middle like a bathmat makes the room look smaller. 160×230cm is the usual minimum for a living room.
- **Washable flatweave** (£30–£50, Dunelm, IKEA, or Ruggable dupes) is the renter's friend — it lifts for cleaning and rolls up when you move.
- Add a thin anti-slip underlay (£8) so it doesn't creep on the carpet.

A rug you actually like on the floor does more for a rented room than almost anything you can hang on the walls.
