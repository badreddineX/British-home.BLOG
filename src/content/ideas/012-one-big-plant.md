A scatter of small supermarket plants on every windowsill tends to look fussy and half of them die by spring. One large plant in the right place does far more for a room.

This week, buy **one floor-standing plant**:

- **Choose for the light you actually have.** A UK north-facing room: a Kentia palm, a ZZ plant, or a cast iron plant. Bright south-facing: a fiddle-leaf fig or a bird of paradise.
- Put it **in a corner beside a chair or the sofa**, or framing one side of a window — where the room currently has dead space.
- A £15 basket or a plain ceramic pot (IKEA, Dunelm) instead of the plastic nursery pot makes it look £60 more expensive than it was.

Water on a fixed day so it's a routine, not a guess. One plant you keep alive beats six you don't.
