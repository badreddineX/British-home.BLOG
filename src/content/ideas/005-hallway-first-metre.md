In most UK flats and terraces the hallway is the narrowest, darkest, most neglected bit of the house — and it's the first thing you and every guest sees.

This week, style just the **first metre inside the door**:

- **A slim console or a narrow shelf** (£30–£50, Argos or IKEA — even 18cm deep works) with a dish for keys and a tray for post.
- **A mirror above it** (£15–£25, Dunelm) — bounces what little light there is and makes the space read wider.
- **Two or three wall hooks** for coats and a bag, plus a basket on the floor for shoes so they're not scattered across the hall.

One warm-bulb lamp or a plug-in wall light finishes it. Narrow, low, and lit — that's the whole formula for a hallway that works.
