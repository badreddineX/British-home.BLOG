The bedside table is the last thing you see at night and the first in the morning, and in most UK bedrooms it's a pile of stuff on the floor or a lamp with a harsh bulb.

This week, spend £30 on the three things that fix it:

- **A warm bulb** — 2700K, "warm white", £3. The single biggest change. Cool-white bulbs are why the room feels clinical at 11pm.
- **A small lamp with a fabric shade** if the current one is bare or plastic (£15–£20, Dunelm, Wilko-type shops, charity shops).
- **A little tray or dish** (£5) so the phone, book, and glass of water have a defined spot instead of sprawling.

If there's no table at all, a stack of three hardback books or a wall-mounted shelf (£10) does the job in a tight box room.
