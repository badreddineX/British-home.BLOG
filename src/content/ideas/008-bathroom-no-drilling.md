UK rental bathrooms are usually a sink with no cabinet, a tiny window, and one wobbly shelf. Everything ends up round the edge of the bath.

This week, add storage that leaves the walls alone:

- **A freestanding ladder shelf or a slim three-tier unit** (£25–£40, The Range, Argos, Dunelm) in the gap beside the loo or basin.
- **A shower caddy that hangs from the showerhead or a tension corner unit** (£8–£15) — gets the bottles off the bath edge.
- **Two or three lidded baskets** on the shelf (£4–£6 each) so backups and clutter are hidden, not lined up on display.

A stick-on LED strip under the mirror (£10) sorts the lighting. Total under £45, all of it moves house with you.
