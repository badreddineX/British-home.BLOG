Most rental kitchens are fine — they're just dated. Two reversible swaps do most of the work:

- **Change the cupboard handles.** A pack of simple brass or matte-black handles is £12–£20 for a whole kitchen. Keep the originals in a drawer, swap them back on moving day. This alone ages a kitchen down a decade.
- **Add a peel-and-stick splashback** behind the hob and sink — tile-effect vinyl, £10–£15 a roll. Wipes clean, peels off without marking the wall.

If there's open shelf or worktop space, a **wooden board and two matching jars** (£8 from a charity shop or Sostrene Grene) give it the "considered" look that expensive kitchens have.

Under £25, one evening, and every bit of it is undoable.
