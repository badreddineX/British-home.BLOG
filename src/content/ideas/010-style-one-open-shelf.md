One well-styled open shelf — in the kitchen, alcove, or hallway — makes a room look finished. One cluttered one makes it look worse. The difference is a few rules, not more stuff.

This week, clear a shelf and rebuild it with:

- **Something tall** on one side — a stack of books stood upright, a jug, a bottle with a single stem.
- **Something round** — a bowl, a small vase, a candle.
- **Something layered flat behind** — a small framed print or a board propped against the wall.
- **Negative space.** Fill about two-thirds; leave the rest empty. That gap is what reads as "styled".

Charity shops and Søstrene Grene are where this gets done for under £20. One good shelf beats five busy ones.
