WEEKLY NEWSLETTER IDEAS
======================

One .md file per weekly email. The broadcast job (api/broadcast.js) sends the
lowest `order:` number that hasn't gone out yet, one per week. Sent ideas are
never repeated (tracked by filename in the `issues` table).

Only .md files are picked up — this .txt is ignored.

FORMAT
------
---
title: "The £20 gallery wall that reads as a full makeover"
order: 1
price: "£20"                                      # optional — badge
image: "/images/patchwork-armchair-gallery-wall.jpg"   # optional — card variant auto-used
relatedPost: "budget-home-makeover-uk"           # optional — "The full guide →" link
---

Body = the tip. 120-170 words, British English, prices in pounds, one concrete
thing to do this week. Plain text. **Bold** and [links](https://britishhomeinterior.co.uk)
work. Blank line between paragraphs.

QUEUE
-----
1  001-living-room-lamp-layers   Living Room
2  002-rented-bedroom-warmth     Bedroom
3  003-rental-kitchen-quick-win  Kitchen
4  004-twenty-pound-gallery-wall Room Makeovers

Add the next as 005-... To reorder, change the `order:` numbers.
