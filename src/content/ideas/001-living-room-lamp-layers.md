The single fastest way to make a British living room feel cosy is to stop using the ceiling light in the evening.

This week: add **three lamps at three different heights** and put them on the same smart plug or a cheap remote socket (£8) so one click turns the lot on.

- A **floor lamp** in the corner behind the sofa — arched or a simple column, £30–£50.
- A **table lamp** on a side table or the media unit — £15–£25.
- Something **low** — a small lamp on a stack of books, or a candle-style bulb in a lantern.

Swap every bulb to **2700K warm white** (£3 each). Cool white is what makes a room feel like an office.

Three pools of low light beats one bright ceiling light every time — and it costs less than a takeaway.
