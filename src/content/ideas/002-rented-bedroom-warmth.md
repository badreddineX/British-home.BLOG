Rented bedrooms are usually magnolia walls, a bare bulb, and a carpet you didn't choose. You can't fix any of that — but you can layer over it.

This week, add **three soft layers**:

- **A large rug** over the landlord carpet (£25–£40 for a washable one). Even a cheap rug you like beats a good carpet you don't.
- **A throw and two textured cushions** on the bed — chunky knit, boucle, linen. Not matching. £8–£15 each from H&M Home or Dunelm.
- **A warm bedside lamp** with a 2700K bulb, so the last light you see at night isn't the ceiling one.

Total under £30, all of it comes with you when you move, and the room goes from "temporary" to "yours" in an afternoon.
